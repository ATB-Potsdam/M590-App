## What is in this build

- **A short page no longer scrolls on mobile.** `body` had `min-height: 100vh`.
  On a phone `100vh` is the viewport height with the URL bar *hidden*, so while
  the bar is visible the body is taller than the screen. The Szenarien page
  therefore scrolled by that difference — a few percent — even though its content
  fills less than half the viewport, and the scroll shadow above the logo bar
  stayed visible the whole time, because `useIsScrolledToBottom()` was correctly
  reporting "not at the bottom".

  Now `min-height: 100dvh`, which tracks the actually visible height, with the
  `100vh` declaration kept ahead of it as a fallback for browsers without `dvh`.

  Reported from a phone; not reproducible on a desktop browser, where `100vh`
  equals `innerHeight` because there is no collapsing URL bar. Measured there:
  `scrollHeight` 679 = `innerHeight` 679, no overflow.

## Not the cause

`.page`'s bottom padding was checked and is correct: 108 px = nav 64 + logo bar
28 + 16 breathing room, matching `--bottom-furniture-height`. This bug predates
the 0.1.49 nav work and the 0.1.51 logo-bar fix.
