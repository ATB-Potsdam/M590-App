## What is in this build

One change, and it moves calculated numbers for everyone — read the next section
before comparing results against an earlier version.

- **Climate data rebuilt from the products the Merkblatt prescribes**
  (`e098334`, web **and** native). Kapitel 4.1.3 names two inputs for the
  Klimatische Wasserbilanz: precipitation from HYRAS and grass reference
  evapotranspiration after **FAO-56** (Allen et al. 1998). The app had been using
  the CDC raster `multi_annual/evapo_p`, which is **AMBAV/Haude — a different
  evaporation model, not FAO-56**. That put two calculations on a basis the
  Merkblatt does not permit:
  - the monthly ΔKWB correction against the Potsdam reference in
    **Gemüse/Obst & Sonderkulturen**, and
  - the ETt evaporation term in **Grünflächen**.

  The KWBv classes A–H of the Hauptkulturen were never affected — they are read
  straight from the Merkblatt's own annex map.

  Neither product is published as a ready-made 30-year monthly mean, so
  `scripts/build_raster_nc.py` aggregates them (~12 GB of daily FAO-56 grids,
  streamed one year at a time). HYRAS turned out to sit on ETRS89-LAEA rather
  than Gauß-Krüger, so **EPSG:3035** was added to `CRS_DEFS`; each raster is
  reprojected from its own `meta.crs`, so the two grids need not agree.

  `scripts/deploy.sh` now refuses to deploy if either raster's `meta.source`
  does not name the prescribed product — the previous mistake was silent.

## Results change, upward

FAO-56 yields systematically higher ET₀ than AMBAV/Haude — 50 to 115 mm more over
March–October depending on the site. The climatic water balance therefore gets
drier everywhere (Potsdam −29 mm, Nürnberg −61 mm, Freiburg −94 mm), and
additional water demand for **Gemüse/Obst & Sonderkulturen** and **Grünflächen**
rises accordingly.

This is a correction, not a regression: the Merkblatt itself notes that the change
of method alone makes many regions one to two KWBv classes drier. Results saved
from 0.1.43 or earlier are not comparable with this version.

**Validation.** The Merkblatt states the monthly KWB for the Potsdam reference
site itself (`refKwb` in `soilConstants.ts`), which makes it a direct test.
Summed absolute deviation, March–October:

| Basis | Σ deviation | exact months | worst month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1.43 (`evapo_p` + `multi_annual/precipitation`) | 27 mm | 0 | 8 mm |
| **0.1.44 (FAO-56 + HYRAS)** | **7 mm** | **4** | **2 mm** |

The app now reproduces the Merkblatt's own reference values; it did not before.

**HYRAS version.** The Merkblatt cites HYRAS v5.0, which the DWD no longer
publishes — the CDC carries only v6.0 and v6.1. This build uses **v6.1**. The two
available versions' dataset descriptions are textually identical (same method,
same Rauthe et al. 2013 reference) and the data differ by ~0.6 % typical, under
1 % of the annual sum at the reference sites.
