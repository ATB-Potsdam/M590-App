## What is in this build

The decisions taken at the working-group meeting on **2026-08-19**. Four points
from the tester correspondence, all of which had been waiting on a ruling we
could not make ourselves. Tracker items 4/21, 5, 14 and 15/22 in
`app/doc/feedback/Feedback-Tracker.xlsx`.

- **Sport, green and golf areas are summed separately** (`236e8d1`). The
  Merkblatt gives them a single Jahresrichtwert and no mittleres Trockenjahr.
  Folding that into the Normaljahr sum labelled it as a scenario value it is not,
  while the Trockenjahr sum silently omitted it — a mixed project's dry-year
  total looked complete when the entire sport block was missing from it.

  There are now three sums: Normaljahr and Trockenjahr over the crop areas, plus
  an own Jahresrichtwert over the scenario-free ones. **Each row renders only
  when it has a value**, so a pure agricultural project looks exactly as before
  and a pure golf project shows only the Jahresrichtwert. Completeness is judged
  per block, so a sport area no longer makes a scenario sum look partial, and
  alternative water is deducted within its own block. The app and the PDF both
  explain why the block stands apart.

  This went **against** the variant proposed to Fiorina Telesca on 2026-08-18
  (carry the values identically in both scenarios); the meeting chose to keep
  them out of both.

- **Deviating from the Tabelle 21 A/J value requires a reason** (`e4b676d`). The
  old 0–40 mm slider merged the table value with a +20 mm surcharge the Merkblatt
  grants only to Hauptkulturen — 40 mm appears in no table. What the Merkblatt
  actually permits is a *justified adjustment*.

  The table value is now adopted automatically when the crop is chosen. A
  deviation sits behind a disclosure and **takes effect only once a reason is
  entered**; until then the table value is what gets calculated *and* saved, so
  an unfinished edit cannot silently move a number. The reason is printed in the
  result card and the PDF, which is what makes the figure defensible in a
  water-rights application.

  Verified end to end: an unjustified 25 mm against a 15 mm table value still
  computed 101–131 mm/a; adding the reason moved it to 111–141 mm/a, exactly the
  10 mm difference.

- **The nav bar moves to the top on desktop** (`ac17cea`). It sits at the bottom
  for one-handed mobile use, but desktop testers kept overlooking it there. Above
  900 px it is now at the top, centred on the page width, with the active-tab
  marker flipped to whichever edge faces the content. Mobile is unchanged.

  One nav instance moved by CSS rather than duplicated — two elements would both
  carry the tour's `data-tour="nav-scenarios"` target. The space freed at the
  bottom edge is expressed once as `--nav-bottom-height`, which the toasts and
  the tour's floating buttons consume instead of each hard-coding 64 px.

- **+20 mm for Kartoffeln is Speisekartoffeln only** (`64550d9`). Confirmed by
  the working group; Verarbeitungskartoffeln get nothing. The +20 mm the internal
  `Funktionsweise3.0.md` listed for them was a transcription error with no basis
  in the Merkblatt. The gate already behaved this way — the rule is now written
  down in `hauptkulturen.ts` so it does not get "fixed" back, and the form states
  what unticking the box means.

Also fixed while in the summary: the horizontal scroll shadow was painted over
the table footnotes, because they sat inside the wrapper carrying the
full-height gradient overlays. Moved out; the shadow now hugs only the scrolling
area.

## What did not change

- **Zwischenfrucht at winter crops** — deferred by the meeting to the December
  group session. `cropAllowsOptionalSurcharge()` still suppresses both surcharges
  for Winterweizen and Winterraps. Do not change it on assumption.
- **Netto-Antragsmenge** still reports the Normaljahr. Whether it should move to
  the mittlere Trockenjahr (Kapitel 3.5) was not on the meeting's decided list
  and remains open; it would move every application's figures.

## Legal status

Meeting agenda items A (who may use the app) and B (reproduction rights for the
tables and the annex climate map) were **postponed — the DWA will discuss them
internally**. Both remain blockers for public promotion. The app stays freely
reachable in the meantime, which is the continuing status quo rather than a
decision that it should stay that way. See `LEGAL-TODO.md`.
