## What is in this build

A regression from 0.1.49, reported from use and fixed here.

- **The logo bar is no longer hidden by the nav on mobile** (`b817e3e`). Moving
  the Betrieb/Szenarien nav into its own fixed wrapper left *two* elements pinned
  to `bottom: 0`. The nav is opaque and stacked higher (z-index 1001 vs 1000), so
  it covered the logo bar outright: on mobile the ATB and DWA logos and the
  „Über die App" / „Impressum" / „Datenschutz" links were simply absent.

  Measured before the fix at 420 px wide: logo bar at y 615–643, nav at y
  579–643 — fully overlapping. After: logo bar 551–579, nav 579–643, and a
  hit-test at the logos' and links' own coordinates returns the logo bar rather
  than the nav.

  This mattered beyond cosmetics: those three links are the Impressum and the
  privacy policy, which have to stay reachable.

- **Floating elements clear the logo bar too** (same commit). The tour buttons
  and the toasts offset themselves by the nav height alone, which placed them on
  top of the logo bar — a hit-test showed „Über die App" being covered by the
  tour button's close control and therefore unclickable, on desktop as well.

  Introduced `--bottom-furniture-height` (nav + logo bar) for anything floating
  above the bottom edge, alongside the existing `--nav-bottom-height` for things
  stacking directly on the nav. Both collapse correctly when the nav moves to the
  top above 900 px and when the logo bar hides on short viewports, which also
  lets `.page` drop two media queries that repeated the same arithmetic.

## Why 0.1.49 shipped with this

The nav change was verified at both breakpoints — that the bar moved to the top
on desktop and stayed at the bottom on mobile, which it did. What was not checked
is what the moved element now *overlaps*: the verification looked at the thing
that changed, not at its neighbours. A hit-test ("is this element actually the
topmost one at its own coordinates?") catches this class of bug and a screenshot
of the changed component does not.
